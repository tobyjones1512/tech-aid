Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

Why is my computer running so slow?

Slow computers are usually caused by too many startup programs, a nearly full hard drive, malware infection, or aging hardware that needs upgrading. We can diagnose and fix slow computer issues with same-day service available in Toronto and Whitby.

How much does computer repair cost in Toronto?

We offer affordable, transparent pricing with no hidden fees. Cost depends on the specific issue - we provide a free diagnosis first so you know exactly what to expect before any work begins. Budget-friendly IT support you can trust.

Is remote tech support safe?

Yes. We use industry-standard, encrypted remote access tools. You can watch everything we do on your screen and disconnect at any time. Remote support is safe, fast, and convenient - great for software issues, settings, and email troubleshooting.

Can you recover my deleted files or data?

In many cases, yes. Data recovery is possible even after accidental deletion, hard drive failure, or system crashes. Contact us as soon as possible - the sooner we start, the better the chance of successful data recovery.

Do you offer tech support for seniors?

Absolutely. We specialize in patient, friendly, non-judgmental tech help for seniors and non-technical users. We explain everything in plain language, at your pace, with no jargon. Tech support shouldn't be stressful.

Do you offer same-day computer repair?

Yes - same-day service is often available in Toronto, Whitby, and the GTA. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll respond quickly. For urgent tech issues, we do our best to get to you or connect remotely the same day.

What's the difference between a virus and malware?

Malware is the broad term for all malicious software - viruses, spyware, ransomware, adware, and more. A virus is one specific type of malware. If your computer is acting strangely, running slowly, or showing unexpected pop-ups, contact us for professional virus and malware removal in Toronto.

Do you support Mac computers as well as Windows PCs?

Yes - we provide tech support for both Mac and Windows computers in Toronto and Whitby. Whether it's a MacBook, iMac, Windows laptop, or desktop PC, we can diagnose and fix it.
